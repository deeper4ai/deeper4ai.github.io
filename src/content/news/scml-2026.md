**[Prof. Cezary Kaliszyk](https://ckaliszyk.github.io/)** gave an invited talk at [SCML-2026](https://scml.risc.jku.at/conference-2026/program/), the conference on Symbolic Computation and Machine Learning, held July 6–8, 2026 in Hagenberg, Austria.

His talk, *"Formalization and Automated Reasoning in the Age of LLMs"*, was presented on Monday, July 6, and discussed how large language models are reshaping formalization and automated reasoning. The [slides are available here](https://scml.risc.jku.at/conference-2026/presentations/scml2026.10a.kaliszyk.pdf).
