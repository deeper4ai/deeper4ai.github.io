Our paper **"Polymorphism Meets DHOL"** by _Rhea Ranalter_, _Florian Rabe_, and _Cezary Kaliszyk_ has been officially published as part of [LIPIcs, Volume 378, FSCD'26](https://www.dagstuhl.de/dagpub/978-3-95977-433-8).

DHOL (Dependent Higher-Order Logic) is a powerful logic with dependent types and strong ATP support. This paper develops polymorphic DHOL (PDHOL), extending the expressivity of DHOL while retaining its simple definition and automation, with a sound and complete translation to polymorphic HOL implemented in a logic-embedding tool.

The paper is available at [doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSCD.2026.27](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSCD.2026.27). See our [Publications](/publications) page for further details and bibtex.
