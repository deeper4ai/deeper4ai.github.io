Two members of our team gave talks at [AITP 2026](https://aitp-conference.org/2026/)
(Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Theorem Proving) on September 1:

+ _Jan Jakubův_ presented **[Learning-Guided Higher-Order Automated Reasoning for Isabelle/HOL](/papers/cicm26B.pdf)**,
  on higher-order extensions of ENIGMA and Deepire evaluated on a large Isabelle/HOL corpus.

+ _Keneni W. Tesema_ presented **[Premise Selection for Higher-Order ATP Problems](https://aitp-conference.org/2026/abstract/AITP_2026_paper_13.pdf)**,
  on selecting relevant premises for higher-order automated theorem proving.
