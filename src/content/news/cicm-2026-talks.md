Our team gave three talks at [CICM 2026](https://cicm-conference.org/2026/cicm.php)
(Conference on Intelligent Computer Mathematics), held in Ljubljana on September 21–25:

+ _Jan Jakubův_ gave an invited lecture
  **A Decade of ATP Tuning: Machine Learning, Strategy Invention, and AI in the Future**
  at the [AI4Math workshop](https://cicm-conference.org/2026/cicm.php?event=ai4math&menu=program)
  on September 21
  ([slides](/papers/cicm26-ai4math-invited.pdf)).

+ _Jeremy Lindsay_ presented
  **[Optimising Metamath Proofs for Human Working Memory](/papers/cicm26A.pdf)**
  (with _Cezary Kaliszyk_ and _Christine Rizkallah_) in the
  [main track](https://easychair.org/smart-program/CICM2026/2026-09-22.html#talk:324161)
  on September 22, on reordering proof steps to reduce the working-memory
  demand on human readers.

+ _Jan Jakubův_ presented
  **[Learning-Guided Higher-Order Automated Reasoning for Isabelle/HOL](/papers/cicm26B.pdf)**
  (with _Cezary Kaliszyk_ and _Martin Suda_) in the
  [main track](https://easychair.org/smart-program/CICM2026/2026-09-22.html#talk:324169)
  on September 22, on higher-order extensions of ENIGMA and Deepire evaluated
  on a large Isabelle/HOL corpus.
