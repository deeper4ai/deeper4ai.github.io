Our paper **Agent Hunt: Bounty Based Collaborative Autoformalization With LLM
Agents**, by _Chad E. Brown_, _Cezary Kaliszyk_, and _Josef Urban_, has been
published in the proceedings of the
[Workshop on Practical Aspects of Automated Reasoning (PAAR 2026)](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4241/),
held as part of FLoC 2026 in Lisbon, Portugal. The paper explores large-scale
autoformalization of algebraic topology using a simulated bounty marketplace,
where multiple LLM agents propose formal lemmas, attach bounties, and compete
to discharge proof obligations, scaling collaborative formalization in a
decentralized fashion.

The paper is available from our [Publications](/publications) page, together
with a link to the arXiv preprint.
