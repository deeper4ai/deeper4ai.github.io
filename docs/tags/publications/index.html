📜 Agent Hunt paper published at PAAR'26

Agent Hunt: Bounty Based Collaborative Autoformalization With LLM Agents, published on CEUR Workshop Proceedings (CEUR-WS.org, ISSN 1613-0073)

 Posted on August 20, 2026  |  Deeper4AI @ Melbourne

Our paper Agent Hunt: Bounty Based Collaborative Autoformalization With LLM Agents, by Chad E. Brown, Cezary Kaliszyk, and Josef Urban, has been published in the proceedings of the Workshop on Practical Aspects of Automated Reasoning (PAAR 2026), held as part of FLoC 2026 in Lisbon, Portugal. The paper explores large-scale autoformalization of algebraic topology using a simulated bounty marketplace, where multiple LLM agents propose formal lemmas, attach bounties, and compete to discharge proof obligations, scaling collaborative formalization in a decentralized fashion.

📚 Two papers published at IJCAR'26

Two papers on uniform interpolation for intuitionistic modal logics, fully mechanised in Rocq, are now published in LNCS, Volume 16688, IJCAR'26

 Posted on August 13, 2026  |  Deeper4AI @ Melbourne

Two papers co-authored by our team member Ian Shillito have been published in the proceedings of the 13th International Joint Conference on Automated Reasoning (IJCAR 2026), held in Lisbon, Portugal, as part of FLoC 2026. Both papers extend Pitts’ proof-theoretic technique for uniform interpolation to new intuitionistic modal settings, and both come with full mechanisations in the Rocq proof assistant.

  • Pitts and Intuitionistic Multi-Succedent: Uniform Interpolation for KM by Hugo Férée and Ian Shillito. Pitts’ technique had so far only been applied to logics on an intuitionistic basis through single-succedent sequent calculi. This paper adapts it to the intuitionistic multi-succedent setting, focusing on the intuitionistic modal logic KM: a novel multi-succedent sequent calculus is designed that terminates and eliminates cut, yielding decidability, and is then used to construct uniform interpolants for KM. By (re)proving the algebraisability of KM, the coherence of the class of KM-algebras follows.

📚 Paper published at FSCD'26

Polymorphism Meets DHOL is now published in LIPIcs, Volume 378, FSCD'26

 Posted on July 15, 2026  |  Deeper4AI @ Melbourne

Our paper “Polymorphism Meets DHOL” by Rhea Ranalter, Florian Rabe, and Cezary Kaliszyk has been officially published as part of LIPIcs, Volume 378, FSCD'26.

DHOL (Dependent Higher-Order Logic) is a powerful logic with dependent types and strong ATP support. This paper develops polymorphic DHOL (PDHOL), extending the expressivity of DHOL while retaining its simple definition and automation, with a sound and complete translation to polymorphic HOL implemented in a logic-embedding tool.
