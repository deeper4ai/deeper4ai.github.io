🗣️ Three talks at CICM 2026

Jan and Jeremy presented DEEPER work at CICM 2026 in Ljubljana, including an invited lecture at the AI4Math workshop

 Posted on September 29, 2026  |  Deeper4AI @ Melbourne & Prague

Our team gave three talks at CICM 2026 (Conference on Intelligent Computer Mathematics), held in Ljubljana on September 21–25:

  • Jan Jakubův gave an invited lecture A Decade of ATP Tuning: Machine Learning, Strategy Invention, and AI in the Future at the AI4Math workshop on September 21 (slides).

  • Jeremy Lindsay presented Optimising Metamath Proofs for Human Working Memory (with Cezary Kaliszyk and Christine Rizkallah) in the main track on September 22, on reordering proof steps to reduce the working-memory demand on human readers.
